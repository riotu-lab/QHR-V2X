## Palettes

Figures use the published colours (A* blue, QHR-V2X red, Dijkstra green). That red/green pair sits at deuteranopia dE 3.9, below the dE 8 target, so `accessible/` repeats Figures 3-8 in Okabe-Ito (dE 11.0, all checks pass). Every figure also assigns a distinct marker per algorithm, so identity never rests on hue alone.
